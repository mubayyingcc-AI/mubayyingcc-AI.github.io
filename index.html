The gap isn't testing. It's accountability.

Most organizations can run a test. The harder question is: once a model, prompt, or policy changes, who is willing to put their name on "this exact version is ready to serve" — and can they show, later, exactly what was checked and why?

In practice, that step often becomes informal, gets skipped under deadline pressure, or gets absorbed into governance work that was never built to re-certify every release. Risk and compliance functions sometimes only get involved after something has already shipped — which is the most expensive place to find a problem.

Mubayyin doesn't replace your governance, your model evaluation, or your risk process. It sits at the one point those processes don't usually cover: the specific, evidenced decision to release this version, right now.

How you can evaluate this without sending us your documents

We don't ask for your policies, customer data, or system configuration before you can understand whether this is useful to you.

  1. Explore it here

    Use the synthetic example above. No account, no documents, no meeting required.

  2. Evaluate with your own sanitized inputs

    Run a small, non-sensitive test case through the engine yourself, in an environment you control. Nothing sensitive has to leave your side to see how it behaves.

  3. Connect a real workflow — only when it's worth it

    If there's a clear fit, we scope a real integration together, under your security and procurement requirements. This step only happens by mutual agreement, not by default.

How we handle your data today

Real, current facts about the v0.1 engine — not a compliance page, and not a certification claim. We don't have those yet, and we'd rather say that plainly than imply otherwise.

The current engine holds every system, scenario, and result in memory only, for the length of a single session. There is no database and nothing is written to disk. When a session ends, that data is gone.

Nothing is sent to a third party. There is no external logging, analytics, or telemetry in this build.

Anything evaluated with us — synthetic scenarios now, sanitized inputs later, or eventually real data under Stage 3 — is never used to train a shared model, and never visible to another customer, without separate, explicit agreement.

We don't ask you to trust us with anything sensitive before you're ready to. That's not a compliance claim — it's how Stage 1 and Stage 2 are designed to work. Why the first two stages need nothing from you

We don't yet hold formal certifications such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001 — this is an early-stage engine. If a real deployment (Stage 3) requires them, that gets scoped together before anything sensitive moves, not asserted here in advance.
